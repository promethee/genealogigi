## Why

Owner feedback after trying the app: several small UI rough edges.

## What changes

- The person form opens with the cursor in the first name.
- The People/Tree switch sits on the left of the toolbar.
- A person's name in the list is plain text (it looked clickable but the View button is the control).
- The share confirmation is a harmless question: green Confirm, neutral Cancel (deletions keep the red Cancel).
- In the small family tree the partner segment has its own colour.
- The app window starts maximized.
