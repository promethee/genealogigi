## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Search people
The app SHALL offer a search box that finds people by name or place; the list shows only matches, the tree highlights them, and choosing a result focuses that person.

#### Scenario: Maiden name
- **WHEN** the user types a maiden name
- **THEN** the person appears in the results

#### Scenario: Tree view
- **WHEN** a query matches people drawn in the tree
- **THEN** their boxes are highlighted
