## Decisions

- **Empty the other field** instead of hiding the chosen person from the other lists: all lists stay complete, so swapping parent and child is one click, and nobody wonders where a name went. Rejected: filtering options (a swap then needs two extra steps).
- **Search filter by involvement**: a line is shown when either person matches; the form's choices are never filtered. The "already partners" check still uses all links.
- **French label**: plural, like the English "Show siblings".
