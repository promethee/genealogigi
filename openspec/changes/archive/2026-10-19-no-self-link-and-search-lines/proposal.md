## Why

A person could be chosen as their own parent, or as both parents, in the relationship form (and as both parents in the new-person form). The search box also left every relationship line on screen.

## What changes

- Choosing someone already picked in another field of the relationship form (or the new-person parents) empties that other field, so no self-link can be added.
- While the search box has text, the relationship list shows only the lines involving a matching person ("Nobody found." when none).
- French "Afficher la fratrie / sororité" becomes "Afficher les frères et sœurs".
