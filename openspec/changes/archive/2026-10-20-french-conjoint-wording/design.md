## Decisions

- **Conjoint(e)** for the person (neutral in practice, covers married and unmarried couples) rather than compagnon/compagne (gendered, excludes married) or couple (awkward as a role).
- **Union** for the link in timelines ("Union avec X : début"), **couples** in the tree legend; plural "conjoint(e)s", agreement in lines uses the generic masculine ("sont conjoints").
- A unit test keeps "partenaire/partenariat" out of the French catalog.
