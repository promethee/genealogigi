## Why
Relatives without the app should be able to browse the family: one file they open in any browser.

- "Share as web page…" in the header writes ONE self-contained `.html` file: people list with search, a card per person (life, notes, photos, relatives as links, timeline), light/dark, works offline and prints.
- A confirmation first warns that the page contains everyone, notes and photos included.
