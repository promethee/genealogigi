## Decisions

- **Not written into the address**: the default person is shown without changing the hash, so `#people` stays a clean link and Back does not gain a step.
- **Wide windows only** (the breakpoint of the two-pane layout, 761 px): on a phone auto-opening a person would skip the list.
- **Seen last, else first**: returning from the Tree restores the person the visitor was on; the first person is the file's first, a neutral pick.
