## Why
"Root person" is unclear and the chart hides distant relatives; users want the whole tree and facts about the person at its centre.

## What Changes
- "Root person" becomes "Focused person" (fr: Personne ciblée).
- Generations gains "All" (default): the whole connected tree is drawn.
- A panel under the controls shows the focused person's numbers: parents, grandparents, great-grandparents, further ancestors, children, grandchildren, great-grandchildren, further descendants, siblings, partners.
