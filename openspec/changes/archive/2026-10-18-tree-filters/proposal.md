## Why
In a big tree it is hard to see, say, who is still alive or who was born in March.

## What Changes
- Filters in the Tree tab: life status (living / deceased), birth place, death place, birth month, death month, last name.
- Filters add up (AND). People who do not match are faded, not hidden: layout, links and clicking stay.
- A match count and a Reset button.
