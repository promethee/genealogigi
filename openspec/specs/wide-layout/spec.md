## Requirements

### Requirement: Use the window width
The people tab SHALL show the people list and the relationships side by side on wide windows and stacked on narrow ones.

#### Scenario: Wide window
- **WHEN** the window is at least 1280px wide
- **THEN** the relationships panel is to the right of the people list
