## Requirements

### Requirement: Quiet defaults
The person card SHALL not show default badges or kinds, and SHALL use singular wording for a single sibling.

#### Scenario: One sibling
- **WHEN** a person has exactly one sibling
- **THEN** the title reads "Sibling" (fr "Frère / sœur")
