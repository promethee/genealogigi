## Requirements

### Requirement: Toolbar order
The People toolbar SHALL show Add person on the left, the search box in the centre and the People/Tree switch on the right.

#### Scenario: Wide window
- **WHEN** the window is wide
- **THEN** the three controls are on one line in that order
