## Requirements

### Requirement: In-app confirmation
Confirmations SHALL appear in an in-app modal styled like the app, answerable by Cancel, Confirm, Escape or backdrop click.

#### Scenario: Cancelling
- **WHEN** the user presses Escape or Cancel
- **THEN** the action is not performed
