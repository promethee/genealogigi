## Requirements

### Requirement: Focused person facts
The tree view SHALL show counts of the focused person's ancestors and descendants per generation, siblings and partners, and SHALL draw all generations by default.

#### Scenario: Counts
- **WHEN** a person has 2 children and 1 grandchild
- **THEN** the panel shows "2 children" and "1 grandchild"
