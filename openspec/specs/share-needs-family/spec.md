## Requirements

### Requirement: Share needs a family to browse
The app SHALL allow sharing as a web page only when the family has at least two people and at least one relationship.

#### Scenario: Too small
- **WHEN** a family has fewer than two people or no relationship
- **THEN** Share as web page is disabled and says what is missing
